# 3. Guardrails & Safety Defenses
**[English](README.md) | [中文](README.zh-CN.md)** · [Back to index](../README.md)

*Detecting jailbreak / malicious prompts, hardening refusal, resolving safety-utility trade-offs, and post-hoc safety interventions on aligned LLMs.*

| Year | Paper | Summary | Venue | Code |
|------|-------|---------|-------|------|
| 2025 | **SoK: Evaluating Jailbreak Guardrails for Large Language Models** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10597)) | **Gap:** The guardrail landscape is fragmented — no unified taxonomy or evaluation framework.<br>**Method:** First SoK on jailbreak guardrails; multi-dimensional taxonomy (6 dimensions) + Security-Efficiency-Utility evaluation framework.<br>**Result:** Maps strengths/limits of existing guardrail approaches, how to optimize them, and universality across attack types. | IEEE S&P 2026 | – |
| 2026 | **Towards Graph-Based Detection of Jailbreak & Prompt-Leakage Attacks (GuardNet)** ([ACM](https://doi.org/10.1145/3805621.3807629)) | **Gap:** Jailbreaks and prompt-leakage share structural manipulation patterns; no unified pre-inference defense.<br>**Method:** Represent each input as a hybrid token graph (sequential + syntactic + attention-derived relations) and classify hierarchically before execution.<br>**Result:** Consistently outperforms existing input-side defenses on jailbreak & prompt-leakage benchmarks. | EuroMLSys 2026 | – |
| 2026 | **LLM-VA: Resolving the Jailbreak-Overrefusal Trade-off via Vector Alignment** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19487)) | **Gap:** Steering the magnitude of answer vectors creates a jailbreak ↔ over-refusal trade-off; root cause is answer-vector and safety-judgment-vector being treated as independent/orthogonal.<br>**Method:** Closed-form weight updates aligning the answer vector onto the safety-judgment vector (per-layer SVM vectors + minimum-norm modification) — no fine-tuning or architecture change.<br>**Result:** +11.45% F1 over best baseline while preserving 95.92% utility; adapts per-model without manual tuning across 12 LLMs. | ACL 2026 | [web](https://hotbento.github.io/LLM-VA-Web/) |
| 2026 | **Bleeding Pathways: Vanishing Discriminability in LLM Hidden States Fuels Jailbreaks (DEEPALIGN)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11185)) | **Gap:** Safety fine-tuning over-rejects benign queries and misses hidden malicious intent because hidden-state separability for safe vs harmful outputs degrades as generation proceeds.<br>**Method:** Contrastive hidden-state steering *mid-generation* (DEEPALIGN) re-amplifies the harmful/benign separation → continuous intrinsic toxicity detection and intervention.<br>**Result:** Cuts ASR of 9 jailbreak attacks to near-zero across diverse LLMs; reduces over-refusal (up to 3.5% lower error on challenging benign queries) with <1% capability decline. | NDSS 2026 | – |
| 2026 | **Unraveling LLM Jailbreaks Through Safety Knowledge Neurons (SafeTuning)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.01631)) | **Gap:** Defenses that modify output distributions / detect harmful content lack a mechanistic rationale.<br>**Method:** Project internal representations into an interpretable vocabulary space to locate safety knowledge neurons; SafeTuning fine-tunes to reinforce them.<br>**Result:** Adjusting safety-neuron activation controls refusal (mean ASR >97% when suppressed); SafeTuning consistently lowers ASR across LLMs, beating 4 baseline defenses. | EACL 2026 | – |
| 2026 | **Knowing-but-Doing: Diagnosing & Defending Role-Play Jailbreaks via Moral Disengagement (MD-Shield)** ([ACL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.349/)) | **Gap:** Role-play safety is under-characterized; models often *recognize* risk in reasoning yet still comply ("Knowing-but-Doing").<br>**Method:** Bandura's Moral Disengagement as an explanatory framework; MD-Trace diagnostic benchmark; MD-Shield introspection-based defense.<br>**Result:** Role-play boosts safety for benign personas but raises unsafe compliance for malicious ones; MD-Shield lowers ASR while preserving role fidelity. | ACL 2026 Findings | [github](https://github.com/lavapapa/MoralJustify/) |
