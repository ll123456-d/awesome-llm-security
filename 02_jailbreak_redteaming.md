# 2. Jailbreak Attacks & Red Teaming

*Attacking aligned (chat) LLMs: adversarial suffixes, semantic / logical / agentic jailbreaks, automated red teaming, and evaluation benchmarks.*

| Year | Paper | Summary | Venue | Code |
|------|-------|---------|-------|------|
| 2023 | **Universal and Transferable Adversarial Attacks on Aligned Language Models (GCG)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15043)) | **Gap:** Jailbreaks require human ingenuity and are brittle; automatic prompt generation had limited success.<br>**Method:** Learn a universal adversarial *suffix* that maximizes the probability of an affirmative response, via greedy + gradient-based search.<br>**Result:** Transferable suffixes jailbreak multiple aligned models (incl. ChatGPT, Bard, Claude) and even work on non-instruction-tuned models. | arXiv | – |
| 2023 | **Jailbroken: How Does LLM Safety Training Fail?** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02483)) | **Gap:** We know jailbreaks exist, but not *why* safety training fails.<br>**Method:** Propose two failure modes — **competing objectives** (capability vs safety conflict) and **mismatched generalization** (safety doesn't transfer where capability does) — and use them to design new attacks.<br>**Result:** New attacks succeed on every prompt in the models' red-teaming eval sets (GPT-4, Claude v1.3), beating ad hoc jailbreaks. | arXiv | – |
| 2024 | **HarmBench: A Standardized Evaluation Framework for Automated Red Teaming** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.04249)) | **Gap:** No standardized framework to rigorously compare automated red-teaming methods.<br>**Method:** Benchmark with 18 red-teaming methods × 33 target LLMs/defenses; introduces a highly efficient adversarial-training defense.<br>**Result:** Large-scale comparison yields novel insights; enables co-development of attacks and defenses. | arXiv | [github](https://github.com/centerforaisafety/HarmBench) |
| 2024 | **Jailbreaking Black Box LLMs in Twenty Queries (PAIR)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08419)) | **Gap:** Automated jailbreaks need gradients or many queries.<br>**Method:** Attacker LLM iteratively refines candidate jailbreaks against a target LLM (social-engineering-inspired), black-box only.<br>**Result:** Often <20 queries to jailbreak; competitive ASR & transferability on GPT-3.5/4, Vicuna, Gemini. | arXiv | – |
| 2024 | **Rainbow Teaming: Open-Ended Generation of Diverse Adversarial Prompts** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16822)) | **Gap:** Adversarial prompt discovery is domain-specific, lacks diversity, or needs heavy human annotation.<br>**Method:** Cast adversarial prompt generation as a *quality-diversity* problem; open-ended MAP-Elites-style search.<br>**Result:** Hundreds of effective prompts, ASR >90% on Llama 2/3; highly transferable; synthetic FT data improves safety. | arXiv | – |
| 2023 | **Catastrophic Jailbreak of Open-source LLMs via Exploiting Generation** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06987)) | **Gap:** Jailbreaks are studied via adversarial *prompts*, ignoring decoding-time manipulation.<br>**Method:** Manipulate *generation strategies* only (decoding hyper-params, sampling methods), no prompt change.<br>**Result:** Misalignment 0%→>95% across 11 models (LLAMA2, VICUNA, FALCON, MPT) at 30× lower cost; proposes a generation-diverse alignment defense. | arXiv | – |
| 2025 | **ASTRA: Automated Strategy Discovery, Retrieval, and Evolution for Jailbreaking LLMs** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02356)) | **Gap:** Existing methods can't continuously learn / self-evolve attack strategies from interactions.<br>**Method:** Closed-loop attack-evaluate-distill-reuse; dynamic 3-tier strategy library (Effective/Promising/Ineffective) as hierarchical memory.<br>**Result:** Significantly outperforms baselines in black-box setting. | arXiv | – |
| 2025 | **AutoDAN-Turbo: A Lifelong Agent for Strategy Self-Exploration to Jailbreak LLMs** | **Gap:** Prior methods need predefined strategy scopes or human intervention.<br>**Method:** Black-box agent that autonomously discovers jailbreak strategies from scratch and reuses them.<br>**Result:** +74.3% avg ASR over baselines; 88.5 ASR on GPT-4-1106; plug-and-play with human-designed strategies (93.4). | (journal) | – |
| 2026 | **RedAgent: Red Teaming LLMs with Context-Aware Autonomous Language Agent** | **Gap:** Prior red teaming ignores context-specific vulnerabilities (e.g., code tasks) and is limited to a few template mutations.<br>**Method:** Abstract attacks into "jailbreak strategies"; multi-agent system with self-reflection + memory buffer that learns context-aware jailbreak prompts.<br>**Result:** Jailbreaks most black-box LLMs within 5 queries (2× more efficient); found 60 real GPTs vulnerabilities. | (journal) | – |
| 2026 | **STAR-Teaming: Strategy-Response Multiplex Network for Automated LLM Red Teaming** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18976)) | **Gap:** Red-teaming search space is high-dimensional and intractable; redundant exploration.<br>**Method:** Multi-Agent System + Strategy-Response Multiplex Network; network-driven optimization organizes search into semantic communities.<br>**Result:** Higher ASR at lower compute than existing methods; adds interpretability of strategic vulnerabilities. | arXiv | [github](https://github.com/selectstar-ai/STAR-Teaming-paper) |
| 2026 | **MetaBreak: Jailbreaking Online LLM Services via Special Token Manipulation** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10271)) | **Gap:** Prompt-engineering jailbreaks don't scale under content moderation; special tokens overlooked.<br>**Method:** Exploit *special tokens* (training metadata) to build 4 attack primitives that bypass both internal alignment and external moderation; evade sanitization via embedding-similar regular tokens.<br>**Result:** Beats SOTA (PAP, GPTFuzzer) by 11.6% / 34.8% under moderation; synergizes with prompt engineering (+24.3% / +20.2%). | S&P | – |
| 2026 | **HarDBench: Draft-Based Co-Authoring Jailbreak Attacks for Safe Human-LLM Writing** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19274)) | **Gap:** LLMs as co-authors can be jailbroken by filling in harmful draft completions — a new, unevaluated vector.<br>**Method:** Benchmark (HarDBench) across high-risk domains; propose safety-utility balanced preference-optimization alignment.<br>**Result:** Existing LLMs highly vulnerable in co-authoring; proposed alignment cuts harmful completions without degrading co-authoring ability. | ACL | [github](https://github.com/untae0122/HarDBench) |
| 2026 | **Logic Jailbreak (LogiBreak): Efficiently Unlocking LLM Safety via Formal Logic** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13527)) | **Gap:** Alignment fails due to distributional gap between alignment prompts and malicious prompts; formal logic unexploited.<br>**Method:** Convert harmful NL prompts into *formal logical expressions* to bypass safety while preserving semantics; build multilingual logic-jailbreak dataset.<br>**Result:** Effective & generalizable across five languages. | ACL | [github](https://github.com/Applied-Machine-Learning-Lab/ACL2026_Logibreak) |
| 2026 | **The Trojan Knowledge: Bypassing Commercial LLM Guardrails via Harmless Prompt Weaving** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01353)) | **Gap:** Prompt-optimization jailbreaks leave detectable malicious semantic signals; deep knowledge structure vulnerability overlooked.<br>**Method:** CKA-Agent frames jailbreak as tree-structured exploration weaving *individually-benign* sub-queries that assemble into harmful knowledge.<br>**Result:** >95% success on Gemini2.5-Flash/Pro, GPT-oss-120B, Claude-Haiku-4.5 even against strong guardrails. | arXiv | [github](https://github.com/Graph-COM/CKA-Agent) |
| 2026 | **The Trojan Example: Jailbreaking via Template Filling and Unsafety Reasoning (TrojFill)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21190)) | **Gap:** White-box not applicable to APIs; black-box optimization yields unnatural / non-transferable prompts.<br>**Method:** Exploit the decoupling of *unsafety reasoning from content generation*: frame malicious payload as a template-filling task for a "safety analysis", hiding intent from classifiers.<br>**Result:** Near-universal bypass: 100% ASR on Gemini-flash2.5 & DeepSeek-3.1, 97% on GPT-4o; interpretable & transferable. | arXiv | – |
| 2025 | **PANDAS: Improving Many-shot Jailbreaking** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01925)) | **Gap:** Many-shot jailbreaking uses random fabricated dialogues; suboptimal.<br>**Method:** Modify dialogues with Positive Affirmation, Negative Demonstration, and topic-adaptive sampling; introduce ManyHarm dataset.<br>**Result:** Significantly beats baselines in long-context scenarios; attention analysis explains the exploit. | arXiv | [github](https://github.com/averyma/pandas) |
| 2025 | **Dialogue Injection Attack: Jailbreaking LLMs through Context Manipulation (DIA)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08195)) | **Gap:** Jailbreak research focuses on single-turn; dialogue-history influence overlooked.<br>**Method:** Inject adversarial *historical dialogues* (gray-box prefilling + deferred responses), black-box via chat API/template.<br>**Result:** SOTA ASR on Llama-3.1 & GPT-4o; bypasses 5 different defenses. | arXiv | – |
| 2026 | **Evolving Jailbreaks: Automated Multi-Objective Long-Tail Attacks (EvoJail)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20122)) | **Gap:** Long-tail distribution jailbreaks rely on handcrafted rules.<br>**Method:** Multi-objective evolutionary search maximizing attack effectiveness & minimizing perplexity; semantic-algorithmic representation of encryption/decryption transforms.<br>**Result:** Discovers diverse, effective long-tail jailbreaks competitive with existing methods. | arXiv | – |
| 2026 | **Conjunctive Prompt Attacks in Multi-Agent LLM Systems** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16543)) | **Gap:** Single-agent safety work misses cross-agent composition; segmented prompts that look benign alone can activate when routed together.<br>**Method:** Routing-aware optimization placing a trigger key (user) + adversarial template (compromised remote agent) across star/chain/DAG topologies.<br>**Result:** Substantially higher ASR than non-optimized baselines at low false activations; evades PromptGuard/Llama-Guard & tool restrictions. | arXiv | [github](https://github.com/UCF-ML-Research/ConjunctiveAgents) |
| 2025 | **Jailbreaking for the Average Jane: Bandit Algorithms for Optimally Enhanced Queries** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.26936)) | **Gap:** Whether *non-expert* attackers can practically jailbreak is unclear — they need a powerful jailbreak + effective malicious query.<br>**Method:** Multi-armed bandit framework to learn the best jailbreak from a large choice set with few queries; curate FRAN (actionable-query) dataset.<br>**Result:** Non-experts can achieve actionable malicious responses with efficient online selection of jailbreaks. | arXiv | – |
| 2026 | **The Art of the Jailbreak: Beyond Binary Scoring (OPTIMUS)** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09225)) | **Gap:** Field lacks large-scale infrastructure + binary ASR can't capture stealth-quality tradeoffs.<br>**Method:** 114k-prompt compositional dataset w/ 14 cyber attack categories; category-aware generator FT; OPTIMUS continuous metric J(S,H) over semantics+harmfulness.<br>**Result:** Reveals a continuous "stealth-optimal" regime (S*≈0.57, H*≈0.43) that binary ASR collapses; enables category-level prioritization. | arXiv | – |
| 2026 | **CCJA: Context-Coherent Jailbreak Attack for Aligned LLMs** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11379)) | **Gap:** Most jailbreak methods use discrete/manual prompt templates; open-source weight access under-exploited.<br>**Method:** Define jailbreak as combinatorial optimization in embedding space of masked LM, balancing ASR with semantic coherence.<br>**Result:** Surpasses SOTA in effectiveness while staying coherent; transferring its prompts to black-box commercial LLMs boosts their success. | arXiv | – |
| 2026 | **TSA: Two-Stage Jailbreak Exploiting Logical Consistency** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11817?)) | **Gap:** LLMs' logical-consistency tendency (once premise accepted, follow the chain) is exploitable.<br>**Method:** Two stages: (1) logic presetting → model writes structured harmful-behavior analysis; (2) intent enhancement → model autonomously produces harmful output.<br>**Result:** 84.83% avg ASR on MiniAdvBench (3.61 QPS); 51.44% on MiniHarmBench across 9 LLMs. | Electronics | – |
| 2025 | **How Do LLMs "Trust" Unknown Knowledge? (Unknown-Knowledge Jailbreak)** | **Gap:** What makes LLMs trust & adopt unknown knowledge (ICL/RAG) is unstudied; exploitable.<br>**Method:** Identify trust factors via 5 pairs of trust-enhancing/diminishing transformations + evidentialism analysis; embed malicious queries in trust-enhanced unknown knowledge.<br>**Result:** 99–100% ASR on all tested LLMs incl. GPT-5.1 in defended & undefended settings. | (journal) | – |
| 2026 | **Jailbreaks as Inference-Time Alignment (LIAR)** | **Gap:** Prior work improves attack effectiveness but gives little explanation for *why* safety alignment fails.<br>**Method:** Frame jailbreaks as inference-time alignment; extend best-of-N to adversarial setting (LIAR); derive Safety-Net measure of vulnerability.<br>**Result:** Competitive ASR with natural, hard-to-detect prompts, running 10–100× faster than suffix-based jailbreaks. | (journal) | – |
| 2026 | **Chasing Shadows: Pitfalls in LLM Security Research** ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09549)) | **Gap:** LLM security research lacks rigor/reproducibility; traditional ML pitfalls predate LLMs.<br>**Method:** Identify nine common pitfalls spanning data collection → pre-training → fine-tuning → prompting → evaluation.<br>**Result:** Provides a validity checklist for LLM security research. | NDSS | – |
